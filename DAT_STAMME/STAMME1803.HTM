  • Zobl Engelbert, born in 1936, an_architect from Zams in Tirol
  • and Brenner Hertha, born in 1938, an_architect of St.Poelten
Los Angeles is the major city in California.

He has a brother Marcus, 3 years and 3 months younger, born in 1971. He is a_Mag.rer.oec. and lives in Wien. They have 2 children: Paul Marius, born in 2004 and Luis Martin, born in 2008.

He is wed in first marriage to Hofmann Elisabeth, a_physician.

He was born on 30.10.1967 in Los Angeles – right next to Sunset Boulevard. Perhaps this is also the reason why he is now an enthusiastic amateur cameraman: The Hollywood air has apparently inspired him already in his pram.

He spent a year of his elementary school years in Basel. His first great love was Lisa – they met in kindergarten in America and were inseparable. One heart and one soul, as they say. He then spent his actual youth in Mauer, later the family moved to Perchtoldsdorf. There he also went to high school. His big dream at the time was to become a pilot. He has passed the entrance exam – in retrospect, perhaps not so bad, because many years later he realized that the profession was not as ideal as he had imagined. So he decided to do something "solid" and started studying business administration. By the way, the enthusiasm for it began early: His Grandma once sent him shopping and gave him a note. When he returned, he suddenly had a lot of coins in his hand. He was convinced that money multiplies – and since that moment he has found economics fascinating.

During his studies, however, he realized that he was particularly interested in psychology and dealing with people. So he specialized more and more in psychology and personnel development and graduated later an apprenticeship as a trainer, moderator and coach. He always wanted to become a consultant or lecturer, but the companies at the time were unanimous in saying that he should first "do something clever" and gain leadership experience. No sooner said than done: he went through several companies. Finally, he applied for a job at Austrian Airlines, stayed there for eight and a half years and spent the last few years working as a manager for the entire Group with his team responsible for strategic personnel development. An incredibly learning time. And above all, he was allowed to be in the cockpit with the pilots.

He then moved to the health sector at the Vinzenz Group, where he was in charge of personnel development and enthusiastically involved in the establishment of a leadership academy. Holding seminars was already was his great passion at the time.

In 2011, he finally dared to take the step into self-employment and founded his own company. The step to become self-employed was not easy for him, because he is an extreme security person. Therefore, he spent over five hours conducting a detailed utility analysis for the decision-making process. The result: 3.39 points for "yes" and 3.41 points for "no". A difference that is hardly measurable. But shortly afterwards, a company called with a large order – and he decided to simply take the plunge and dare to become self-employed. To this day, he is very happy to be a lecturer, consultant and process facilitator. It's not just his job, it's really his calling.

Away from work, he is an enthusiastic Jaguar driver. A dream car – but unfortunately also a dream in terms of repair costs. But the joy still prevails. He also loves camera work, has completed an apprenticeship and is always working on joint projects with his son Paul. Maybe one day a small production company will emerge from it. Another hobbyhorse is dancing: standard and Latin. He was already in the gold course, realistically he is currently somewhere between bronze and silver - but with unchanged enthusiasm.

His two sons mean a lot to him. He has a wonderfully warm relationship with Luis, who is still at school. Paul is already active in the media industry, was even a television announcer – of which he is very proud as a father.

As far as his partnerships are concerned, he is separated from the mother of his children. Today he has a very dear partner, and is grateful for the time together. So all in all, a life between Sunset Boulevard, Swiss elementary school, Perchtoldsdorf, flying dreams, psychology, cameras, jaguar, dance floor and two great sons.
