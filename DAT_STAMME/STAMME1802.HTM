Zams in Tirol is a village in Tyrol near Landeck.

He had 2 bretheren: Gerhard, born in 1942 and Arthur, born in 1932. He married at the age of 30 years in the year 1966 in Deutsch_Wagram Brenner Hertha -, 1 years and 11 months younger -, an_architect, born in 1938 from St.Poelten. He is an_architect and the couple lives in Perchtoldsdorf. They have 2 children: Thomas, born in 1967 and Marcus, born in 1971.

He saw the light of day on All Saints' Day, on 1.11.1936 and spent the first nine years of his life in Ried, Oberinntal. Until graduating from secondary school, he lived in Reutte, Außerfern.

From 1951 to 1957 he attended the HTL for building construction in Innsbruck and graduated in the summer of 1957. Master School Dr. Roland Rainer, Vienna and graduated with distinction.

Subsequent years of practice took him abroad, first to Basel and then to Copenhagen, to one of the best international architects. From 1964 to 1966 he returned to Vienna to study with Prof. Rainer.

In 1965, after an Austria-Hungary football match, he met his great love Hertha at a party. After being offered a scholarship at UCLA (Los Angeles) in the spring of 1966, the young couple married in German-Wagram (Hertha's hometown at the time). After a short honeymoon, Bert traveled to the USA to improve his English skills before starting his studies. Hertha did not immediately receive a visa and was able to follow Bert to the USA with the help of a celebrity visa.

It didn't take long before the first son Thomas was already born.

He completed his studies with a master's degree and worked for 5 years with internationally renowned architects and was awarded the Progressive Architecture Awards several times. In 1971, his second son Marcus was born in Hollywood. He then worked for 2 years as head of design for a large architectural firm in Basel.

In 1974, the Zobl family returned to Vienna. From 1974 to 1979 he was a project designer at PVA, Handelskai. In 1979, the Zobl family moved to Perchtoldsdorf, Lower Austria, where the couple lives happy and satisfied to this day - from 1979 he worked as an independent architect. From 1979 to 1982 he was a partner in the office of Lippert, Burckardt & Partner. From 1974 to 2015, he built many buildings as an independent architect in various working groups, with Hertha always being at his side as a professional partner. Together, the couple has achieved numerous competition successes.
