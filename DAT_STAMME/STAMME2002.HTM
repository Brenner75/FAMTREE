Poole is a seaside town on the southern coast of England and you may look at some photos here.

She has 2 bretheren: Samuel Peter Karl, born in 1992 and Charlie Tristan, born in 1993. She is a_health care assistant and lives in Poole. They have a only daughter Millie M. Maggie, born in 2022.

She is partner from MacGregor William Gow, a_butchery assistant.

She was born in Poole Hospital and grew up in Parkstone. She attended Heatherlands First School, then Baden Powell St Peters middle school and finally Poole High School, where she attended Sixth Form too. After finishing her A-levels, she still couldn‘t decide if she wanted to go to university or defer for a year, so after applying to 5 different universities for Adult Nursing, she decided to hold off and started working as a Healthcare Assistant for her mother's care agency. After a year, she decided to permanently work for a small care home in West Moors called Denewood House. After a couple of years, she completed her NVQ level 3 and was promoted to a Senior Healthcare assistant. In 2016 she met Will, and after discovering they had the same taste in music and generally enjoyed each other‘s company, she decided he was the one. This became offical at her Mum and Dad‘s 25th wedding anniversary party, where her Godmother asked Will who he was to Alex and he confirmed he was her boyfriend. From there they moved into a small flat in Parkstone, until moving to Canford Heath and starting a family together.

Now, she is completing her NVQ level 4 and thoroughly enjoys working with the Elderly, as well as being a mother to her daughter. On the family holiday to Croatia in 2025, Will finally proposed to her in the Ampitheatre in front of everyone, and luckily (for him) she said YES!

And like all Askells she loves her curries, which she could eat for years!
