Askell Samuel was born in the zodiac sign of Capricorn on a Tuesday, the 06.01.1992 in Poole as son of
  • Askell Jonathan Frank, born in 1971, a_sales assistant from Poole
  • and Brenner Martina Helene, born in 1970, a_housewife and shop manager of Wien
Poole is a seaside town on the southern coast of England and you may look at some photos here.

He has 2 bretheren: Charlie Tristan, born in 1993 and Alexandra Francesca, born in 1998. He married at the age of 32 years on a Tuesday, the 06.05.2024 Grint Leah Marie - 6 months younger -, an_Environmental Consultant, born in 1992 from Swansea. He is a_technical account manager and the couple lives in Clevedon. 11 months later had her daughter Lowri, born in 2025.

He was born in Poole hospital. He attended Heatherlands First School, then Baden Powell St Peters middle school and finally Poole Grammar school where he attended Sixth Form. After questionable A Level results he was lucky to be admitted to Aberystwyth University to study Business Management with Mathematics. In his second year he dropped Mathematics to major in Business Management. After university Sam moved to Birmingham and got a job as an employee relations assistant at CrossCountry Trains and began his railway career. He was succesful on an operational graduate scheme at the company before moving to TTG Transportation as a Support Manager harnassing driver advisory systems for the industry. He joined the civil service in 2022 as a rail strategy and operations manager in the Department for Transport. In January 2016, Sam joined the Army Reserves at B Squadron, the Royal Yeomanry, a light cavalry reconnaisance regiment working off the Jackal platform. Sam was fortunate to travel with the army throughout his career, the highlights being Australia, Cyprus and France where he met the (then) Prince Charles III. He succesfully promoted to Lance Corporal in 2020, Corporal in 2022 and Sergeant in 2025.

Sam met his wife to be Leah, on the App Bumble and they had their first date in January 2018. Despite both taking long trips to Australia and America at the start of their relationship they were both very keen to keep seeing each other, and became officially a couple when climbing Ben Nevis in Scotland on their first road trip. They loved to explore travelling on holidays together and undertaking adventures including Park Run and Bike rides. They moved into together in July 2019 in a lovely flat in Royal Leamington Spa. They survived the COVID lockdowns together and decided they wanted to buy a house closer to their families, purchasing their first home in Clevedon, North Somerset in March 2022 (80 miles exacty to both parents!). Sam proposed to Leah in July 2022 on her 30th birthday celebration, by asking all her friends to hold up a sign. Following more holidays and a lovely wedding day in May 2023, Sam and Leah welcomed their first child in April 2025 Lowri Askell.

When he was quite young he joined his Opa on a boat trip on the Nile, where he saw the boat from Agatha Christie's film 'Death on the Nile'.

And like all Askells he loves his curries, which he could eat for years!
