Dean Chloe was born in the zodiac sign of Libra on a Saturday, the 26.09.1987 in Bournemouth. Bournemouth a noble seaside resort in the South of England.

She is a_Mental Health Nurse and lives in Poole. They have a only Son Dylan Karl, born in 2022.

She is partner from Askell Charlie Tristan, a_Gas Engineer.

She was born in Boscombe, Bournemouth, in September 1987, the daughter of Deanna Yvonne Dean and Craig Anthony Dean. Boscombe Hospital was demolished a few years later. When she was five or almost six years old, she became a big sister to Alana Nicole Dean, born in August 1993. Chloe attended Kings Park Primary School and then Bournemouth School for Girls. As a child, she enjoyed sports and gymnastics, where she won a number of medals. She also loved athletics – especially the sprints. At school, Chloe particularly enjoyed studying science, English literature and history, achieving 10.5 GSCES and then 3 A-Levels and 2 AS-Levels. Chloe struggled to decide between a career as a history teacher or nurse, but eventually her caring nature prevailed and she decided to follow in her mother's footsteps and become a nurse, but unlike her mother, she specialized in mental health care – she graduated in 2011 and then worked in both psychiatric inpatient and community hospitals (but preferred the latter and so she ended up specializing in it). As adults, her interests included festivals, films and long walks in beautiful natural landscapes, especially the coast and the mountains. On Boxing Day 2019, she met Charlie through her sister Alana at a party at her house. The two enjoyed the outdoors together and had a wonderful time together – and in November 2022, they welcomed their son Dylan, who is the best thing that ever happened to them!
