He was a_politician.

The later privy councillor Smutny was as eccentric and miserly as his parents. He was supposed to be paired up with Alice, but she didn't want to know anything about him. It was all over - when she learned during a visit to his parents' house that he had to sleep with stubble between his fingers to promote his dexterity for playing the piano.

He was often invited to the Binder family, but did not appreciate the excellent food ("they could also cook me a piece of wood, it would taste the same"), nor did he ever bring presents, not even when he was invited to weddings. Even as a privy councillor in the state government, he was not above scrounging cigarettes from everyone.

He turned down an invitation to visit his "childhood friend" after the war, because he was afraid that the Russians could tear his clothes off his body.

The exact date of death is not known, but he died propably at the age of 80 years in the year 1965.

He lived in the epoch of Historicism and as a young fellow under the reign of the Emperor Franz-Joseph I of Habsburg-Lothringen. Some of his contemporaries of a similar age are Konrad Adenauer, Albert Einstein and Franz Marc. During his life there were many wars, amongst them the Great war, the Second World war and the Korean war. Amongst important discoveries and inventions during his time are the motorbike, the coffee filter, the 'Goldbaerchen' sweet and the compact camera.
