The exact date of birth is not known, but Roeckl Anna was propably born 1875 in Hohenthan. Hohenthan lies in Bavaria north of Landshut.

She was married to Franz Josef -, 7 years older -, born in 1883 from Hohenthan. They had 2 children: Dorothea, born in 1914 and Hans, born in 1925.

The date of death is not known.

She lived in the epoch of Historicism and as a young girl under the reign of the Emperor Franz-Joseph I of Habsburg-Lothringen. Some of her contemporaries of a similar age are Marie Curie, Rosa Luxemburg and Mata Hari.
