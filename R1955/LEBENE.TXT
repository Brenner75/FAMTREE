He was a childhood friend of *r54*Albrecht Mock*, with whom he later sang together in the Vernay Quartet and gave guest performances throughout Europe and Turkey.
He was also a great admirer of *r55*Emilie Binder*, whom he met during his military service in +oBADEN+Baden+. 
But his friend *r54*Albrecht Mock* then made the race for *r55*Emilie*.
Professionally, he was very successful and became General Manager of the Austrian Tobacco Company. He was responsible for buying tobacco all over the world. Besides, he cared a lot for his workforce and built the first social housing for them. In several cities he was made Honorary Citizen and streets and squares were named after him, for example in +oSCHWAZ+Schwaz+ in TIrol.
In the end, he was knighted  by Emperor Franz Josef I.
He lived in Vienna in an apartment in Reichsratstrasse, where there was a bell for the servants in every room. It was rumoured that he dated a tobacco sales woman during his wife's lifetime. 
Later he adopted the grandchild of his *r55*childhood sweetheart*  *r1816*Hertha Seil*, who had been widowed very early in life. He made many trips with her, especially to +oMUENCHE+Muenchen+, for shopping. 
Since he was very rich, he also bought her two apartments in Vienna's first district, which are still owned by the family today.
When he died, he almost got a state funeral. It took 12 wagons to transport the wreaths alone.
